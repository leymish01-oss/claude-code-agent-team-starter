## Goal
Get the first 10 paying customers for a small web tool that turns CSV exports into clean charts.

## The one metric that matters this week
Signups from people who aren't friends or family. Latest real value: see `metrics/` (unknown until logged).

## Current bet
- **What:** a free tier (3 charts/month) and a $9/month plan.
- **Who:** freelancers who send monthly reports to clients.
- **Why it can win:** the two tools they use today need a spreadsheet detour; ours takes one upload.
- **Kill criteria:** fewer than 5 signups after 300 landing-page visits → change the pitch or the audience.

## Constraints
- About 5 hours a week of owner time. No paid ads until there is one paying customer.
